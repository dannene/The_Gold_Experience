INTRODUCTION

JoJo's Bizarre Adventure is a series that tells the story of the Joestar family across multiple generations. Each storyline introduces a new main character with their own personality, challenges, and adventures. The series combines action, tons of unique characters, creative battles and a very distinctive style.

I started watching JoJo's Bizarre Adventure in 2020, when I was 15 years old. Since then, the series has remained a big part of my life. What initially started as simply watching a silly anime has grown into a huge part of my personality and influenced me in many different ways over the years.

I've gone through many different phases and interests when it comes to media, but JoJo's Bizarre Adventure has always remained a constant. It's the only series I've remained genuinely interested in for such a long time.

HIROHIKO ARAKI

Hirohiko Araki is the creator of JoJo's Bizarre Adventure. His distinctive art style, unusual character designs, and creative storytelling have made his work stand out.

His art style incorporates inspiration from various forms of art, fashion, music, and pop culture, which is reflected throughout JoJo's Bizarre Adventure.

One thing I admire about Araki's work is his unconventional approach to character design and anatomy. He often goes against typical standards, incorporating high fashion, androgynous characters, dramatic poses and experimental colour choices.

Araki challenged traditional shonen gender expectations through characters like Jolyne Cujoh. Introducing characters whose personality and appearance aren't defined by traditional ideas of how a female character should look or act. In my opinion his willingness to experiment and challenge conventions is a big part of what makes his work so interesting.

His artistic vision is a big part of what makes the series so recognizable and unique. His work has introduced me to countless artists, musicians and fashion influences that I might not have discovered otherwise.

ARTWORK

One thing that often comes up when discussing JoJo's Bizarre Adventure is its art style. It's no secret that Araki's unconventional approach to character design and anatomy is a reason for some people to hesitate to get into the series.

While this can be off-putting to some, it's actually one of the things I find most appealing about the series. I love how different it looks, and I think its visual identity is a huge part of what makes JoJo so iconic.

I've always enjoyed drawing myself, and Araki's work is one of my biggest inspirations. One of my goals is to eventually be able to create art pieces with the same level of detail, creativity, and artistic expression as his.

I still have a lot to learn and a long way to go, but his work continues to motivate me to improve and develop my own style.

MUSIC

Hirohiko Araki often incorporates references to musicians, bands and songs into his characters, their abilities and storytelling. I personally find this aspect of the series especially interesting as it has introduced me to a lot of music that I now enjoy on a daily basis. On this page I want to highlight some of my favourite musical references within JoJo's Bizarre Adventure and share a little about them.

Giorno Giovanna: Gold Experience

To start of with my Digital Garden its main inspiration, Giorno Giovanna who is the main character of part 5 his ability is named after the Prince album “The Gold Experience”.

Risotto Nero: Metallica

Risotto Nero is a minor villain in part 5 and has the ability called “Metallica” which is currently one of my favorite bands, who ive discovered through his character.

Yoshikage Kira: Queen and David Bowie

Yoshikage Kira, the main villain of part 4 has multiple references to Queen, specifically: Killer Queen, Sheer Heart Attack and Another One Bites the Dust. His character design was also based on David Bowie.

Josuke Higashikata: Prince and Crazy Diamond

Josuke Higashikata the main character of part 4 his character design is based on Prince. His ability named Crazy Diamond, references Shine On You Crazy Diamond by Pink Floyd.

Rohan Kishibe: Heaven's Door

A character that starts of as a minor side villain, later becomes a major side character and ends up as the main character of his own spin-off. Rohan Kishibe his ability references Knockin' On Heaven's Door by Guns N' Roses.
